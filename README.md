SwasthyaSetu — Connected Care Platform
SwasthyaSetu is a smart rural healthcare infrastructure designed to eliminate structural inefficiencies like episodic care, invisible capacity, frontline isolation, zero accountability, and excessive patient cognitive load. Focused around the Choubeypur/Varanasi context, it offers dedicated portals for patients, community health workers, doctors, and district administrators.

🚀 Key Features & Architectural Pillars
Longitudinal Care Continuity: Replaces one-off episodic visits with a unified patient profile and a continuous Care Journey timeline.

Real-Time Capacity & Demand: Tracks facility availability, generic medicine stocks (via Jan Aushadhi integration), and active diagnostic slots.

Frontline Worker Empowerment: Connects community health workers with centralized logs, automated triage, and follow-up loops.

Accountability Loops: Automated referrals, follow-up reminders, and digital status tracking across sub-centres, PHCs, CHCs, and District Hospitals.
